# NeuronBridge Nexus

NeuronBridge Initiative (NBI) is a developing UCLA student organization centered around neuroscience, premed, community service, neurodiversity outreach, clinical experiences, mentorship, and community. The organization is being built to help students explore neuroscience beyond the classroom while gaining meaningful service, leadership, professional development, and healthcare-related experiences.

The website should serve as the main home for NBI where students can learn about our mission, our six pillars, upcoming events and meetings, service and clinical opportunities, neurodiversity outreach projects, mentorship and professional opportunities, publications, and ways to get involved.

I also want a section specifically for students interested in becoming founding members and eventually pursuing leadership positions as we build the organization. Visitors should be able to join our interest list, contact us, follow our social media, and learn about upcoming opportunities.

The organization is currently in its founding stage and is working toward becoming a fully registered UCLA student organization.

Visually, I want the website to feel like a vintage neuroscience or medical journal but still modern, elegant, professional, and easy to navigate. Use warm ivory/cream backgrounds, deep burgundy accents, charcoal text, elegant serif typography, subtle paper textures, and vintage anatomical neuroscience illustrations such as neurons, brain anatomy, scientific diagrams, DNA, and medical imagery. Avoid the typical bright blue healthcare/science website aesthetic. The design should feel academic, sophisticated, slightly old-world, and consistent with NeuronBridge Initiative's existing branding.
